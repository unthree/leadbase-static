Morning Routine

Create before you consume. Every goal owns a slot.

  1. ActivateWake up · drink water · make bed — one block, no phone
  2. P90X workoutEnergy generator — keep it early
  3. Film a video contentPeak energy, zero inputs. Topic was decided last night.
  4. Email block — 20 min, timer on emailReply under 2 min · archive · or defer to task list. Inbox zero.
  5. One revenue action revenueFollow up a lead, send a pitch, publish an offer — one per day
  6. Twitter — post first, 15 min, timer onPost a clip from today’s video, reply to comments, out
  7. ReadFeeds tomorrow’s video ideas
  8. Journal + planWrite tomorrow’s video topic and revenue action — pre-decide
Weekly scoreboard: videos filmed · inbox-zero mornings · revenue actions taken. If a number stalls, fix that slot — don’t overhaul the routine.
